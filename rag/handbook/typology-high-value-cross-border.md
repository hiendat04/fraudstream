# High-value and cross-border payments

Large payments and payments abroad carry more risk, but most of them are genuine:
people travel and buy expensive things.

## Signs

- An amount of 500 USD or more, especially far above the customer's 30-day average.
- A merchant in a different country from the customer's home country.
- A payment at night in the customer's home time zone.
- A high-risk merchant: one whose prior fraud rate over 30 days is well above its
  category's.

## What to check

Check whether the customer has paid in that country before, and whether earlier
payments on the trip were card-present. A genuine traveller usually shows
card-present payments in the new country before an online one.

## What to do

Release when the customer confirms the trip, or when earlier card-present payments
in the same country show it. Otherwise call the cardholder. Never block a
traveller's card without trying to call first: it leaves them with no way to pay
abroad.
