# Card testing

Card testing is a fraudster checking whether stolen card numbers work by making
small payments before a large one.

## Signs

- Many payments on one card within minutes, often for small amounts.
- A burst of declined payments followed by an approved one.
- Payments at many different merchants in a short time, or many small payments at
  one online merchant.

## What to check

Compare the customer's payment count and distinct merchants over the last 7 days
with the last 30 days. Look at the 5-minute window: payment count, declined count
and distinct merchants. A sudden jump against the customer's own history matters
more than the raw number.

## What to do

Hold every later payment on the card until the cardholder confirms. If the
cardholder does not recognise the small payments, block the card and refund them
as fraud. Flag the merchant when several tested cards used it.
