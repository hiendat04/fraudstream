# Contacting the cardholder

## When to call

Call the cardholder about any held payment of 500 USD or more, and about any
payment where the evidence does not explain the score. Use only the phone number
on file, never a number given in the payment details or by a caller.

## Checking who is on the phone

Ask for the date of birth and the last four digits of the card. Never ask for the
full card number, the CVV, a PIN, a password or a one-time code. A real bank never
needs them on a call, and asking teaches customers to give them away.

## Asking about the payment

Read out the merchant, the amount, the time and the country. Ask whether the
customer made the payment, whether anyone else uses the card, and whether the
customer has lost the card or phone recently.

## When nobody answers

Leave a message asking for a call back on the number printed on the card, with no
payment details in it. Try again after two hours. After 24 hours without contact,
follow the blocking rule in the review policy.
