# Account takeover

An account takeover is a fraudster getting into a real customer's account and
paying as that customer.

## Signs

- A payment from a device the customer has never used.
- No strong authentication: no 3-D Secure, no one-time code, no biometric check.
- A large payment or a cash transfer soon after the new device appears.
- Payments between midnight and 05:00 in the customer's home city.

## What to check

Check when the device was first seen on the account and which authentication the
payment used. Compare the amount with the customer's 30-day average. Check whether
the customer's contact details changed recently.

## What to do

Hold the payment and call the cardholder on the number on file. If the customer
did not make the payment, block the card, sign out every device and ask the
customer to change the password in the app, never over the phone.
