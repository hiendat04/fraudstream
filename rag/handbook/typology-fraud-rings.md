# Fraud rings

A fraud ring is a group of fraudsters who reuse the same devices and network
addresses across many stolen cards.

## Signs

- One device id or IP address used by several unrelated customers within days.
- Payments at electronics, cash transfer, online marketplace or travel merchants,
  whose goods resell easily.
- Online or mobile wallet payments with no strong authentication.
- Several cards used for the first time at the same merchant.

## What to check

Look up the device and IP address of the held payment and count how many other
customers used them in the last 30 days. Check whether those customers have
confirmed fraud. Check the merchant's prior fraud rate over 30 days.

## What to do

Block every held payment from the shared device. Escalate to the fraud operations
lead when five or more customers share one device or IP address. Do not call the
customers before the lead decides: a call can warn the ring.
