# Handling customer data

## Card numbers

Never write, paste or send a full card number. Use the last four digits. The
copilot's gateway masks card numbers in questions, but a case note or a ticket is
not masked.

## What goes in a case note

Facts and decisions only: signals, evidence, decision, time. No opinions about the
customer, no health or family details, and no passwords or one-time codes, even if
the customer reads them out.

## Sharing outside the team

Case details leave the fraud team only through the case system: to the police on a
formal request, or to the card network for a chargeback. Never by personal email or
chat.

## How long data is kept

Case notes are kept for five years after the case closes, as card network rules
require. Call recordings are kept for 90 days.
